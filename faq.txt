What plans are available?
We are currently offering Basic, Standard and Premium plans.

How can I check coverage in my area?
On our homepage, if you click the Check Coverage button in the left panel, you will be able to see if
we cover your area.

How do I pay? or How to select payment method?
We accept both Cash on Delivery and Mobile Banking. Upon selecting your desired Internet plan, you
will be 
automatically directed to the payment option where you can select your preferred payment option.

How to apply promotions?
After selecting your desired Internet plan when you proceed to payment, you will see a designated box
for promo code. You will enter your promo code there and get discount. You can find promo codes from 
promotions on the left panel of our homepage.
